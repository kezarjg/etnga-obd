## Notes

This is the **Central Gateway (CGW1N02) heartbeat** broadcast — well-established in the opendbc Toyota DBC (`BO_ 1114 CGW1N02` = decimal 1114 = `0x45A`). The single decoded signal is `CGW2REV`, the gateway's firmware revision code at byte 0.

- Always present, always at ~1 Hz, always the same payload while the car is parked. The cycle time is rock-solid 1000 ms ±1 ms.
- Cycles even when most other ECUs are quiet — this is a useful "is the gateway alive" indicator for passive monitoring.
- Bytes 1-7 are documented as padding/reserved upstream, but the constant `00 A6 02` triplet shared with `0x4E0` is suggestive of a shared header field across gateway-originated broadcasts.
- Originally documented here at `confidence: low` with three `Unknown_b*` signals; reconciled 2026-05-10 against the opendbc Toyota community DBC.
