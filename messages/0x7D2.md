## Notes

The diagnostic-request side of the EV ECU's UDS-over-ISO-TP channel.

**Polling cadence under different Techstream screens:**
- Idle / vehicle-info screen: polls `0xF186` every ~2 s as session keep-alive.
- Data List with all items enabled: cycles through ~218 unique source DIDs at high rate (~10 polls/sec aggregate). Most polls go through dynamic slots `F301`/`F302` after a big `0x2C 01` setup.
- Data List with one item enabled: polls `F301` (and sometimes `F302`) at ~12 Hz, with a fresh `0x2C 01` define payload identifying the source DID.

**Standard "Techstream changes Data List" sequence on the wire:**
```
3E 00            TesterPresent
10 01            DiagnosticSessionControl → defaultSession
10 03            DiagnosticSessionControl → extendedDiagnosticSession (required for 0x2C)
31 03 10 02      RoutineControl → requestRoutineResults, routine 0x1002 (purpose unknown)
2C 03 F3 01      Clear dynamic DID slot F301
2C 03 F3 02      Clear dynamic DID slot F302
2C 01 F3 01 ...  Define F301 (multi-frame; source DID specs)
2C 01 F3 02 ...  Define F302 (multi-frame; second batch of source DIDs)
22 F3 01         Begin polling slot 1
22 F3 02         Begin polling slot 2
```

**ISO-TP framing:** standard, no extended addressing. Tester sends flow control `30 00 01` (continue, no block size limit, 1 ms separation time).
